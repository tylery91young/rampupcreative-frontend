Where to propose

Favorite proposal spots in Utah County

A few places that photograph beautifully. Have your own spot in mind? I'll go anywhere in Utah County and beyond.

Foothill overlooks

The ridges above Provo, Orem, and Alpine look out over Utah Valley and Utah Lake — this is where I proposed. Unbeatable at sunset.

Provo Canyon & Bridal Veil Falls

Waterfalls, river trails, and canyon walls just minutes from BYU and UVU. Gorgeous year-round, especially in fall color.

Sundance & the Alpine Loop

Aspens, mountain lodges, and the backside of Timpanogos. Peak autumn here is one of the prettiest backdrops in the state.

Utah Lake at sunset

Wide-open shoreline and huge skies — a perfect canvas for drone shots of the two of you as the sun goes down.

Rock Canyon & Y Mountain

Classic Provo trails with dramatic cliffs and city views — a favorite for BYU couples.

Cascade Springs & Tibble Fork

Boardwalks, turquoise pools, and mountain reservoirs for couples who want something a little off the beaten path.

Heads up: drones aren't allowed everywhere (wilderness areas, some parks, airspace near Provo Airport). As a Part 107 certified pilot I handle the airspace rules — if a spot isn't drone-friendly I'll tell you up front and suggest one nearby that is.

FAQ

Proposal photography questions

How do you stay hidden during a surprise proposal?

We scout the spot ahead of time and pick a hiding place — behind trees, posing as a hiker, or simply far back with a long lens. The drone can fly high and wide enough that it blends into the background. You'll know exactly where I am; your partner won't.

Won't the drone give it away?

Not if we plan it right. I put the drone up before you arrive and keep it at a distance where it's quiet and easy to miss — plenty of hikers fly drones in the Utah mountains. In my own proposal, my fiancée had no idea until I was on one knee.

Where in Utah do you shoot proposals?

All of Utah County — Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Alpine, Highland, Saratoga Springs, Eagle Mountain, Springville, Spanish Fork, Mapleton, and Payson — plus the canyons and mountains in between. Salt Lake and Wasatch County are on the table too; just ask.

Do you also do engagement photos?

Yes. Most proposals roll straight into a short engagement session while the light is good, and I also book standalone engagement sessions for couples who are already engaged.

What do I get afterward?

A gallery of edited, high-resolution photos, and — if you add video — a cinematic proposal film in widescreen plus a vertical cut ready for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

What if the weather is bad?

Utah weather changes fast. If it's unsafe to fly or the conditions would ruin the photos, we'll pick a backup time or a nearby backup spot together — no stress.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know your date — evenings at golden hour fill up, especially in fall. Last-minute requests are welcome too; reach out and I'll do everything I can to make it work.
