FAQ

Proposal photography questions

How do you keep it a surprise?

The drone does the work. I launch before you arrive and stay out of sight while it films from a distance, so there's no photographer standing around to tip anyone off. You'll know where I am; your partner won't.

Is everything shot with a drone?

Yes. The proposal video and the photos are all shot with my drone. That's what makes it different: views of the two of you and the whole valley that a camera on the ground can't get.

Won't the drone give it away?

Not if we plan it right. I put the drone up before you arrive and keep it at a distance where it's quiet and easy to miss — plenty of hikers fly drones in the Utah mountains. In my own proposal, my fiancée had no idea until I was on one knee.

Where in Utah do you shoot proposals?

Anywhere in Utah County where drones are allowed to fly — Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Springville, Spanish Fork, and beyond. The Kyhv Peak overlook above Provo is a favorite. Many popular spots (wilderness areas, some parks, certain airspace) are off-limits, so I'll confirm your location is legal before we plan anything.

Do you also do engagement photos?

Yes — aerial engagement photos. Most proposals roll straight into a short drone photo session while the light is good, and I also book standalone drone engagement sessions for couples who are already engaged.

What do I get afterward?

Your proposal edited into a cinematic drone film in widescreen, plus a vertical cut ready for Instagram Reels and TikTok, and a gallery of edited, high-resolution drone photos from right after the yes.

What if the weather is bad?

Utah weather changes fast. If wind or weather makes it unsafe to fly, we'll pick a backup time together — no stress.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know your date — evenings at golden hour fill up, especially in fall. Last-minute requests are welcome too; reach out and I'll do everything I can to make it work.
